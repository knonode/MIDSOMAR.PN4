Welcome to your own MIDSOMAR manager! Here are the rules:

  • NOTE: Combining combined assets is not possible atm
  • Base assets (MS 00-76) can only be combined in groups of 1, 3, 7, 11, or 13 sequential numbers
  • When assets are combined, they are burned and create a new asset named after their range (e.g., MS 0103 for combining MS 01 through MS 03)
  • The "Simulate Shuffle" button randomly distributes assets
  • Fill in Asset index and amount to burn
  • Orange rows indicate assets you currently own
  • Columns are sortable
  • The Range column shows what base assets were combined to create that asset
  • The Factor column shows how many assets were combined (and their sub-factors in parentheses)
